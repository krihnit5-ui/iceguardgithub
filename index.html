AI / ML decision layer

Iceberg trajectory & anomaly intelligence

Observed iceberg positions remain the source of truth. IceGuard adds a transparent AI anomaly detector and a short-horizon wind-forced trajectory nowcast; modelled outputs are never labelled as observations.

How the AI layer works

Observed iceberg + weather→Feature vector→kNN anomaly score→Trajectory nowcast

Features are observed latitude/longitude plus wind speed, wind direction (sine/cosine) and pressure from the nearest monitored site. A k-nearest-neighbour distance model flags unusual combinations without inventing observations.
